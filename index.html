Hanish Suri among top global technology and AI leaders honoured as a Distinguished AI Innovation & Research Fellow

A global recognition celebrating visionary leadership, responsible innovation and a sustained commitment to advancing artificial intelligence for meaningful impact.

EmergeAI Editorial TeamAugust 5, 2026 · 5 min read
Sharein✉

Hanish Suri has been recognised among an eminent group of global technology and artificial intelligence leaders, receiving the distinction of AI Innovation & Research Fellow.

The honour recognises leaders whose work moves artificial intelligence beyond experimentation and into responsible, real-world application. It celebrates a career shaped by curiosity, practical innovation and the conviction that technology should expand human potential.

“

Innovation becomes meaningful when it is inclusive, responsible and designed to improve the way people live and work.

Leadership at the intersection of technology and impact

As an IT Director at Boston Consulting Group and Senior Technical Advisor at EmergeAI Technologies, Hanish has championed an approach to technology and artificial intelligence that combines ambitious innovation with measurable outcomes. His leadership is grounded in transforming complex enterprise challenges into dependable systems, clearer decisions and sustainable business impact.

The fellowship also acknowledges his contribution to the wider AI community—connecting research, enterprise and emerging talent while advocating for thoughtful governance and human-centred design.

Distinguished FellowshipRecognising global contribution to AI innovation, research and responsible technology leadership.

Building the next chapter of responsible AI

This recognition arrives at a defining moment for the industry. As AI becomes embedded in everyday products and decisions, the need for transparent, dependable and inclusive systems has never been greater.

Hanish’s continued focus is on turning that responsibility into action: supporting research, mentoring innovators and helping organisations adopt AI with both confidence and care.
